How scoring works

Win = 3 points
Each member of the winning pair receives 3 tournament points.
Loss = 1 point
Each member of the losing pair receives 1 tournament point.
Tie-break = capped point difference
Cap each match's point difference at ±5 for 21-point games or ±3 for 15-point games.
Final ranking
1) Tournament points. 2) Cumulative capped point difference. If still tied, players share the position.
Match score
First to exactly 21 or 15 wins; no two-point margin is required.
